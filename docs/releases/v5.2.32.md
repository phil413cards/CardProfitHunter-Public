# CardProfitHunter V5.2.32

## Summary

V5.2.32 prevents card accessories, empty packaging, and card-related services
from being treated as purchases of a specific raw card.

## Included

- Excludes card display stands, frames, binders, storage boxes, and holders sold
  by themselves
- Excludes supply products such as sleeve lots, toploader packs, and team bags
- Excludes empty wrappers, packaging-only, label-only, COA-only, and no-card
  listings
- Excludes card grading, cleaning, authentication, restoration, consignment,
  and submission services plus replacement labels
- Applies the existing non-card merchandise boundary during classification,
  search filtering, Scout ranking, and direct financial analysis
- Rechecks listing text at the final Scout boundary instead of trusting
  precomputed actionable metadata
- Applies an independent direct financial-engine veto even when an unsafe
  valuation keyword exactly repeats the accessory or service wording
- Extends the direct-engine boundary to existing non-card objects such as
  signed baseballs
- Preserves ordinary protection wording on real card listings, including
  `Ships In Toploader`, `With Penny Sleeve`, and `One Touch Included`

## Safety Boundaries

This release changes non-card merchandise detection only. It does not change
money formulas, valid raw-card identity matching, eBay requests, database
behavior, CSV exports, dashboard filtering, stored user data, or runtime
configuration.
