# CardProfitHunter V5.2.27

## Summary

V5.2.27 prevents randomized card products from appearing as Scout candidates or
receiving financial recommendations.

## Included

- Adds a dedicated non-actionable classification for mystery boxes, packs,
  cards, hits, lots, bundles, and repacks
- Excludes seller repacks, grab bags, blind bags or packs, random cards, hits,
  players, teams, packs, and lots
- Excludes surprise cards or products plus hot and chase packs
- Removes affected rows during search relevance filtering and before Scout
  analysis
- Rechecks listing text at the final Scout boundary instead of trusting
  precomputed actionable metadata
- Applies an independent direct financial-engine veto even when an unsafe
  valuation keyword exactly repeats the randomized-product wording
- Preserves specific-card wording such as `Chase Card`, `Case Hit`, `Randomly
  Inserted Refractor`, `Pack Fresh`, and `One Of One`
- Prevents a serial number such as `/10` followed by `Pack Fresh` from being
  misclassified as a ten-pack product

## Safety Boundaries

This release changes randomized-product eligibility and one pack-context parser
edge case only. It does not change money formulas, valid card identity
matching, eBay requests, database behavior, validation, exports, dashboard
filtering, or stored user data.
