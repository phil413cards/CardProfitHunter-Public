# CardProfitHunter V5.2.25

## Summary

V5.2.25 prevents listings without an immediately available, inspectable actual
card from appearing as Scout or financial opportunities.

## Included

- Adds a dedicated non-actionable classification for presale, pre-sale,
  preorder, and pre-order listings
- Excludes not-in-hand, ships-when-received, ships-upon-release, and
  not-yet-released wording
- Excludes stock, sample, example, and representative photos or images
- Excludes listings that say the actual card is not pictured or that the image
  may vary
- Removes affected rows during search relevance filtering and before Scout
  analysis
- Rechecks the listing title at the final Scout boundary instead of trusting
  precomputed actionable metadata
- Applies an independent direct financial-engine veto even when an unsafe
  valuation keyword exactly repeats the listing wording
- Preserves safe `in hand`, `ready to ship`, `actual card pictured`, image/photo
  variation, and `pack fresh` wording

## Safety Boundaries

This release changes listing-availability and image-evidence eligibility only.
It does not change money formulas, valid card identity matching, eBay requests,
database behavior, validation, exports, dashboard filtering, or stored user
data.
