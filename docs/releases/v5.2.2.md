# CardProfitHunter V5.2.2

## Summary

V5.2.2 delivers the Search Intelligence and Hybrid Scout milestone.

## Included

- Player-title relevance filtering for live eBay searches
- Exclusion of lots, breaks, sealed products, digital cards, and reprints
- Hybrid Scout candidates when no exact verified valuation exists
- Clear separation between verified recommendations and unverified discovery candidates
- Search-stage diagnostic counters
- Duplicate DataFrame column protection
- Hard exclusion of hats, caps, apparel, figures, posters, and other non-card merchandise
- Preservation of legitimate refractors, parallels, autographs, and jersey-patch cards
- Correct singular and plural live-search result messages

## Financial Safety

Unverified Scout candidates remain PASS and are not presented as confirmed BUY or OFFER recommendations. They require:

- Recent sold-comparable research
- Exact card and parallel confirmation
- Front-and-back photo inspection
- Seller and return-policy review
