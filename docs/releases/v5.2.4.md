# CardProfitHunter V5.2.4

## Summary

V5.2.4 improves verified card-identity matching for common, benign eBay title variations while preserving conservative rejection of conflicting or insufficient identities.

## Included

- Allows limited title variation only when year, product/set, card number, player name, and material parallel or variant identity are strongly anchored
- Accepts selected harmless presentation wording, seller stock codes, omitted trailing team context, and team-city qualifiers
- Continues rejecting conflicting sets, years, card numbers, print runs, parallels, variants, teams, players, slabs, lots, and unknown modifiers
- Prefers the most specific identity rejection reason when one candidate is clearly closest
- Records a read-only production eBay search audit and focused post-fix re-audit
- Adds regression coverage for audited Wembanyama, Ohtani, and LeBron title patterns

## Financial Safety

A title-level identity match is decision support, not proof that the physical card is correct. Actionable rows still require a current verified valuation and complete eligible listing data. Operators must verify photos, condition, authenticity, seller terms, shipping, and sold comparables before acting.

Unverified Scout candidates and rejected identities remain PASS and expose no valuation, profit, ROI, max-buy, or suggested-offer fields.

## Verification

- 187 tests passed
- 300 production Browse API listings reviewed in a read-only controlled audit
- Intended title-level identity matches improved from 2 to 15 in the audited sample
- Zero observed wrong valuation attachments
- Zero observed financial-field leaks on PASS rows
- No purchase, offer, bid, listing change, or database write was performed during the audit

These audit results describe a limited sample and do not guarantee future matching accuracy or profit.
