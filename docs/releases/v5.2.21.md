# CardProfitHunter V5.2.21

## Summary

V5.2.21 prevents card-brand words from making obvious sports memorabilia look
like raw cards or unverified Scout opportunities.

## Included

- Adds strong object-level exclusions for signed balls, 8x10 photos, helmets,
  jerseys, bats, gloves, footwear, pucks, coins, and books
- Applies those exclusions even when a title also contains Topps, Panini, Upper
  Deck, a parallel term, a serial number, or an autograph signal
- Removes branded memorabilia during relevance filtering and before Scout
  analysis
- Verifies the final Scout boundary rejects spoofed actionable metadata
- Preserves explicit card constructs including signed baseball cards,
  jersey-patch autos, coin cards, photo variations, baseball and bat relic
  cards, and dual-relic booklets

## Safety Boundaries

This release changes discovery classification only. Excluded memorabilia stays
non-financial, and verified valuation matching, money math, eBay requests,
database behavior, validation, exports, and stored user data are unchanged.
