# CardProfitHunter V5.2.3

## Summary

V5.2.3 strengthens live-search safety and prevents ambiguous or multi-card listings from being presented as grading candidates.

## Included

- Rejects plus-separated multi-card bundles
- Rejects condition-ambiguous listings such as `(READ)`, `PLEASE READ`, `SEE DESCRIPTION`, and `AS IS`
- Preserves legitimate dual-autograph, dual-relic, and booklet cards
- Preserves harmless marketing wording such as `Refractor + Free Shipping`
- Corrects the Financial Pass metric
- Renames the live export to Download Search Results CSV
- Adds regression tests for mixed-card and condition-ambiguous listings

## Financial Safety

Unverified Scout candidates remain PASS and are not presented as confirmed BUY or OFFER recommendations. They require:

- Recent sold-comparable research
- Exact card and parallel verification
- Front-and-back photo inspection
- Seller and return-policy review
