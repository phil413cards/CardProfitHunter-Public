# CardProfitHunter V5.2.20

## Summary

V5.2.20 prevents sealed products and multi-card inventory from appearing as
raw single-card or unverified Scout opportunities.

## Included

- Classifies retail, hanger, display, booster, hobby, blaster, and mega boxes
  or cases as sealed products
- Excludes sealed and unopened packs, product tins, and empty packaging
- Classifies complete sets, factory sets, quantity-based card sets, bundles,
  and collections as multi-card listings
- Removes these product types during search relevance filtering and before
  Scout analysis
- Reclassifies titles at the final Scout boundary instead of trusting stale or
  spoofed actionable metadata
- Preserves legitimate single-card wording such as `pack fresh`, `pack pulled`,
  `fresh from pack`, and `box topper card`

## Safety Boundaries

This release changes discovery filtering only. Excluded rows remain
non-financial, and verified valuation matching, money math, eBay requests,
database behavior, validation, exports, and stored user data are unchanged.
